MOD · 01 Route Optimization BFS · DFS · A*

Find the optimal shipping route between Bangladesh cities using three graph search algorithms. BFS finds fewest stops, DFS explores deep paths using recursion, A* finds shortest total distance using heuristics.

MOD · 02 Fleet Scheduling Genetic Algorithm

Schedule 2 delivery vehicles across 4 cities using a Genetic Algorithm. The algorithm evolves over 80 generations using Selection, Crossover, and Mutation to minimize total travel distance.

MOD · 03 Drone Frequency Zoning Graph Coloring

Assign radio frequencies to drone delivery zones using Graph Coloring. Adjacent zones with overlapping signals must receive different frequencies to avoid interference. The greedy algorithm minimizes total frequencies used.

Zones: Z1, Z2, Z3, Z4, Z5
Conflicts: Z1↔Z2, Z2↔Z3, Z3↔Z4, Z1↔Z3, Z4↔Z5
MOD · 04 Contract Bidding Minimax · Alpha-Beta

Simulate competitive corporate bidding using Minimax with Alpha-Beta Pruning. The AI evaluates all possible outcomes to recommend the most profitable bid strategy.

MOD · 05 Warehouse Management Backtracking

Place goods safely in warehouse zones using Backtracking. Hazardous materials (Chemicals, Explosives, Flammables) cannot be placed in adjacent zones.

⚠️ Hazardous: Chemicals, Explosives, Flammables  |  Max 4 items (4 zones available)

MOD · 06 Risk & Delay Prediction Bayesian Network

Predict operational delays and risks using a Bayesian Network. Select the current conditions to calculate the probability of delays and estimated extra costs.
